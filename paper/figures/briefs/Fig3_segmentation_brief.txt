FIGURE 3 — ANGIOGRAPHIC SYNTHESIS AND VASCULAR MASKING (simplified)
====================================================================
LaTeX label: fig:segmentation
Status: designer — two real images embedded in a schematic flow.
Recommended size: two-column, ≈1400 × 480 px, 300 dpi.
Placement: after the angiographic-synthesis and segmentation paragraphs.

PURPOSE
-------
Show in one compact figure: (i) how A(x) and the GT mask M are derived
from the 3T input, and (ii) how M is then used as training labels for
two nnU-Net ResEnc-M models that produce inference masks for native 3T
and downsampled 0.5×3T volumes respectively.

LAYOUT — TWO CONNECTED STAGES
------------------------------

Three zones left-to-right connected by white arrows (►).

  Zone A1 — Input (~20 %):
    Single image: fig3_proj_3T_mag.png (3T magnitude Z-MIP, grayscale).
    Label below: "3T 4D Flow  magnitude MIP"

CAPTION DRAFT
-------------
"Angiographic synthesis and vascular masking. The 3T temporal magnitude
MIP is multiplied voxel-wise by the normalised speed projection to form
the angiographic volume A(x). A binary CoW mask M is derived by
intensity thresholding, bootstrap nnU-Net segmentation, and manual
correction of the 7T reference, then used as ground-truth labels to
train two nnU-Net ResEnc-M ensembles: one on native 3T MIP projections
(Dataset 301, cross-validated Dice 0.83) and one on 0.5× downsampled
3T MIP projections (Dataset 306, Dice 0.76). The resulting inference
masks define the fluid region for the SR loss and for all evaluation
metrics."
