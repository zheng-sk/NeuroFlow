FIGURE 5 — LOSS PARTITION AND EVALUATION REGIONS
================================================
LaTeX label: fig:loss
Status: schematic + small qualitative panel.
Recommended size: 2-column, ≈1800 × 800 px, 300 dpi.
Placement: after the loss-function and evaluation-metric equations.

PURPOSE
-------
Visualise where each loss term is evaluated and where each reported
metric is computed. The figure justifies the mask-aware split of
L_vel (fluid vs background), L_mag (inside the mask only), L_TV
(outside the mask only) — and then shows the predicted-vs-reference
surfaces that produce Dice, sMSD and HD.

LAYOUT — TWO PANELS SIDE BY SIDE
--------------------------------
Panel (a) "Loss partition" (left, ~55 % width):
  A representative axial slice from one subject (post-registration 3T
  magnitude) in grayscale. Overlay the binary CoW mask M(x) as a
  semi-transparent blue region (interior) and the complementary
  outside region as a semi-transparent grey wash. Annotate with:
    - Blue interior  →  arrow labelled "L_vel (fluid)  ·  L_mag"
                        leading to a small equation card showing
                        the masked fluid MSE expression.
    - Grey exterior  →  arrow labelled "λ_bg · L_vel (bg)  +  L_TV"
                        leading to a second card showing the TV
                        expression with the joint-outside indicator
                        Ω(x, d).
  In a corner of the panel, list the loss weights as a tiny inset
  table: λ_bg = 0.1, λ_M = 1, λ_TV = 1e-5.

Panel (b) "Evaluation regions" (right, ~45 % width):
  A small 3D rendering of the CoW vasculature for the SAME subject.
  Show two surfaces in close-up around the MCA bifurcation:
    - S_R (7T reference surface) drawn in solid grey.
    - S_P (predicted surface obtained by re-segmenting the network
      output with the ResEnc-M ensemble) drawn in translucent red.
  Add three small graphical annotations:
    - A green double-headed arrow between the surfaces labelled
      "Dice (volumetric overlap)".
    - A blue arrow at a typical boundary location labelled
      "d(p, S_R) → sMSD".
    - A bold red arrow at the locus of the largest local disagreement
      labelled "max d(·) → HD".
  Beneath panel (b), a compact legend strip with the three metric
  symbols (Dice ↑, sMSD ↓, HD ↓) and their units.

CAPTION DRAFT
-------------
"Loss partition and evaluation regions. (a) The CoW mask M separates
fluid voxels — where L_vel(fluid) and L_mag drive learning and where
the intraluminal MSE is later reported — from extraluminal voxels,
where the background velocity term λ_bg · L_vel(bg) and the
total-variation penalty L_TV act. (b) Vascular geometry is evaluated
by re-segmenting the predicted velocity volume with the same
ResEnc-M ensemble (Fig. 3) and comparing the resulting surface S_P
to the 7T reference surface S_R, yielding Dice, sMSD and HD."
