FIGURE 7 — INFERENCE AND QUALITATIVE RECONSTRUCTION
===================================================
LaTeX label: fig:inference
Status: designer-only (built from real LOOCV predictions).
Recommended size: full-page, ≈1800 × 2200 px, 300 dpi.
Placement: at the end of the Methodology, immediately before the
Results section.

PURPOSE
-------
Make the reconstruction quality tangible. On a single held-out
subject from the LOOCV protocol, show side-by-side velocity maps,
voxel-wise error maps, 3D streamlines, and the re-segmented
predicted CoW surface overlaid on the 7T reference surface used to
compute Dice / sMSD / HD. The figure connects the methodology to
every metric in the Results tables.

LAYOUT — FOUR SUB-PANELS
------------------------
Panel (a) — Velocity vector maps at mid-systole (top row, ~40 %
height):
  Three columns × three rows: rows are axial, coronal, sagittal
  through the same anatomical landmark (e.g. the MCA bifurcation);
  columns are
    Col 1 "3T input" — velocity vectors drawn as small arrows
    coloured by speed (viridis colormap) overlaid on the grayscale
    magnitude.
    Col 2 "Network prediction" — same visualisation for the CBAM-DNS
    (× 1) AND a small inset showing CBAM-SupRes (× 2) so that both
    task configurations are represented.
    Col 3 "7T reference" — same visualisation for the registered
    7T volume.
  Shared horizontal colorbar showing speed range in cm/s. Yellow
  arrows on Col 2 pointing to a small vessel that is correctly
  recovered.

Panel (b) — Voxel-wise relative speed error maps (middle row,
~20 % height):
  Two columns showing |Prediction − Reference| / |Reference| inside
  the CoW mask for the × 1 (CBAM-DNS) and × 2 (CBAM-SupRes) outputs,
  overlaid on the angiographic volume A(x). Shared colorbar in the
  range [0, 1] (clipped per voxel as described in the Methods).

Panel (c) — 3D streamline rendering (bottom-left, ~25 % width,
~40 % height):
  Streamlines of the predicted velocity field within the CoW mask,
  coloured by speed, integrated from seed points distributed along
  the proximal MCA and ACA. A second smaller streamline render
  immediately below shows the 7T reference streamlines for the same
  seed set. Camera pose identical between the two renders.

Panel (d) — Re-segmented predicted vs reference CoW surface
(bottom-right, ~75 % width, ~40 % height):
  3D rendering of two overlaid surfaces for the SAME subject:
    - The 7T reference surface S_R in solid neutral grey.
    - The predicted surface S_P (obtained by passing the predicted
      magnitude through the ResEnc-M ensemble) in translucent red.
  A zoomed inset focuses on the MCA bifurcation. Annotate the sub-
  panel with the actual Dice, sMSD (mm) and HD (mm) for that
  subject as a small white-on-dark text card in the corner so the
  reader can map the visualisation directly onto the corresponding
  Results-table row.

CAPTION DRAFT
-------------
"Inference and qualitative reconstruction on a representative
held-out subject from the LOOCV protocol. (a) Mid-systolic velocity
vector maps for the 3T input, the CBAM-DNS / CBAM-SupRes prediction,
and the 7T reference in three orthogonal planes through the MCA
bifurcation. (b) Voxel-wise relative-speed error inside the CoW
mask for the × 1 and × 2 outputs, overlaid on the angiographic
volume. (c) 3D streamlines of the reconstructed velocity field
compared against the 7T reference. (d) The re-segmented predicted
CoW surface (red) overlaid on the 7T reference surface (grey);
per-subject Dice, sMSD and HD are reported on the inset card and
match the corresponding Results-table row."

TYPOGRAPHY & PALETTE
--------------------
Sans-serif. Velocity colormap: viridis. Error map: magma or inferno.
Streamlines coloured by speed (viridis). Surfaces: neutral grey
reference, red (#B85C6B) prediction. Yellow arrows for callouts.
