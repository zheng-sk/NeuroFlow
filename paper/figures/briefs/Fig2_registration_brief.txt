FIGURE 2 — MOTION CORRECTION AND INTER-SCAN REGISTRATION
========================================================
LaTeX label: fig:registration
Status: designer-only (needs cohort data + registration outputs).
Recommended size: 2-column, ≈1800 × 1100 px, 300 dpi.
Placement: after the registration equations.

PURPOSE
-------
Demonstrate visually that (i) intra-scan rigid motion correction
aligns the cardiac-phase frames within each acquisition, and (ii) the
rigid + affine inter-scan registration brings 7T volumes into voxel-
level correspondence with the 3T frame, which is a prerequisite for
supervised learning.

LAYOUT — TWO ROWS
-----------------
Row 1 (intra-scan motion correction, ~35 % height):
  Four small axial magnitude panels of a SINGLE subject's 7T (or 3T)
  acquisition across four representative cardiac frames (e.g. end-
  diastole, mid-systole, end-systole, late-diastole). Above each
  panel write the frame index. Below the row of frames, draw a left-
  to-right arrow labelled "rigid T_t^rigid" leading to a second row of
  the SAME frames after motion correction; on a thin overlay (red
  contour) on each post-correction frame, draw the vessel-edge contour
  taken from the reference frame to show coregistration. Caption strip
  below row 1: "Intra-scan rigid motion correction aligns each cardiac
  phase to the reference frame."

Row 2 (inter-scan 7T→3T registration, ~65 % height):
  Four equally sized panels in a horizontal strip:
    (a) "3T temporal MIP" — coronal or axial MIP of the 3T magnitude,
        grayscale.
    (b) "7T temporal MIP — before registration" — same orientation,
        grayscale; this should obviously misalign with (a).
    (c) "7T temporal MIP — after rigid + affine registration" —
        registered 7T MIP in the 3T grid, grayscale.
    (d) "Checkerboard overlay" — a checkerboard composition of (a)
        and (c) showing alignment quality at the vessel boundaries.
  A 1 cm white scale bar on every panel. Optional fifth small panel
  to the right: a heatmap of the displacement-magnitude field (mm)
  derived from the affine transform, with a vertical colorbar.

CAPTION DRAFT
-------------
"Motion correction and inter-scan registration. (Top) Intra-scan rigid
correction of the cardiac-phase frames; red contour shows the
reference-frame vessel boundary overlaid on each post-correction
frame. (Bottom) Temporal MIPs of the paired 3T and 7T magnitude
before and after the rigid + affine 7T→3T registration, with a
checkerboard overlay illustrating voxel-level alignment at the
Circle of Willis."

TYPOGRAPHY & PALETTE
--------------------
Sans-serif. Panel titles 11 pt bold; sub-labels 9 pt. Magnitude in
grayscale; reference-frame overlay in red (#B85C6B); checkerboard in
default grayscale tiles. Displacement-magnitude heatmap in viridis.

DATA NEEDED
-----------
- One representative subject (preferably the same as Fig. 1 for
  continuity).
- Four cardiac-phase magnitude frames before and after intra-scan
  rigid motion correction.
- Pre- and post-registration 7T temporal MIPs together with the 3T MIP
  in identical orientation.
- Displacement-magnitude volume (optional).

NOT TO INCLUDE
--------------
No patient identifiers, no scanner serials, no acquisition timestamps.
