FIGURE 1 — ACQUISITION AND PAIRING OVERVIEW
===========================================
LaTeX label: fig:acquisition
Status: designer-only (needs real anonymised data).
Recommended size: 2-column journal width, ≈1800 × 900 px, 300 dpi.
Placement: after the cohort paragraph in the Methodology.

PURPOSE
-------
Show how each subject contributed paired 3T and 7T 4D Flow MRI within a
single imaging session, and convey the qualitative SNR / resolution
gain at 7T that motivates the cross-field-strength learning task.

LAYOUT — TWO ROWS
-----------------
Row 1 (per-subject pairing schematic, ~25 % of figure height):
  A horizontal banner. On the left, a stylised patient icon labelled
  "n = 7 subjects". An arrow leads to a single horizontal "session
  timeline" where two scanner icons appear consecutively: a 3T Siemens
  scanner (left) and a 7T Siemens scanner (right). Between them write
  "same session". Above each scanner, list the acquisition output as a
  small stack of four boxes labelled "M, u, v, w" (one magnitude
  volume + three velocity components). Beneath each scanner, note the
  number of cardiac frames (3T: 9–14 frames; 7T: 10–15 frames) and the
  common VENC = 0.9 m/s.

Row 2 (imaging comparison, ~75 % of figure height):
  Two side-by-side panels for the SAME subject and SAME anatomical
  slice. Panel (a) "3T magnitude" — representative axial slice through
  the Circle of Willis; Panel (b) "7T magnitude" — the same slice from
  the within-session 7T acquisition. To the right of each axial slice,
  a small inset shows a sagittal MIP of the whole volume to convey 3D
  coverage. Use a shared grayscale window. Add a 1 cm white scale bar
  in the bottom-right of every panel and yellow arrows pointing to a
  small distal vessel that is visible at 7T but blurred at 3T.

CAPTION DRAFT
-------------
"Acquisition and pairing overview. For each of the seven subjects a
paired 4D Flow MRI study was acquired on a clinical 3T and an
investigational 7T Siemens system within a single imaging session,
sharing a common VENC of 0.9 m/s. The representative axial slice
illustrates the SNR and resolution gain at 7T that the network learns
to recover from the 3T acquisition."

TYPOGRAPHY & PALETTE
--------------------
Sans-serif throughout. Panel titles 11 pt bold above each image;
annotations 9 pt. Use dark blue (#4F7AA8) for the 3T branch and teal
(#5C9E7E) for the 7T branch in the schematic banner; grayscale for
imaging panels. Yellow arrows on the imaging panels.

DATA NEEDED
-----------
- One representative subject's 3T and 7T magnitude volumes after
  DICOM→NIfTI conversion (no preprocessing necessary).
- The same axial slice (matching world coordinate after the
  inter-scan registration of Section 2.2) for both modalities.
- Sagittal MIPs of the two volumes.

NOT TO INCLUDE
--------------
No patient identifiers, dates, or device serial numbers. No coloured
overlays on the imaging panels (kept grayscale on purpose).
